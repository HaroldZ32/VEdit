## Findings
- byte_col equals the independently computed byte offset for every row (byte columns are exact).
- editor character (0-based UTF-16) equals the independent UTF-16 offset for every row.
- display column differs from the 1-based UTF-16 column on 7 of 10 rows:
    undeclared_tab: display=21 vs utf16(1-based)=18
    undeclared_cjk1: display=14 vs utf16(1-based)=13
    undeclared_cjk2: display=16 vs utf16(1-based)=14
    undeclared_cjk3: display=18 vs utf16(1-based)=15
    undeclared_e1: display=13 vs utf16(1-based)=14
    undeclared_e2: display=14 vs utf16(1-based)=16
    undeclared_e3: display=15 vs utf16(1-based)=18
- Therefore display columns cannot be mapped back to text offsets without a
  width table identical to slang own (tab=8, CJK=2, emoji=1, combining=0);
  the runner asks for byte columns instead and converts exactly.
