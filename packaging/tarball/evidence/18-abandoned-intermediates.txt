# the frozen commit. Two candidates failed one of those tests; neither is shipped.

  provenance : build/artifacts/vd-app, mtime 2026-10-30 13:12:09, consumed by the
               AppImage build (packaging/appimage/build-appimage.sh) and found
               inside the AppImage at usr/bin/vd-app.
  rejection  : it predates the last commit that touches crates/vd-app/src

               binary of the *same* size, so 809cf953 cannot be reproduced from
               the frozen commit. It may well contain the fixes (it renders
               screenshots), but a release artifact must be traceable to the
               frozen source; this one is not.
